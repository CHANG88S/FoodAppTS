About BuhEats

BuhEats lets you rate the specific menu items you love (or regret) — from boba sweetness levels to late-night eats — discover restaurants through your community, and share quick reviews with other food lovers.

Common Questions

  • How do I report inappropriate content or a user? Use the report button on any review, post, profile, or comment. Our moderators review every report, and content that violates our rules is removed.
  • How do I block another user? Open their profile and tap the block option. You won't be able to interact with each other; their posts won't appear in your feed.
  • How do I delete my account? Go to Account Settings in the app and choose delete account. All your personal data and content are removed from our servers.
  • How do I change my taste preferences? Open the profile drawer and replay the taste tour, or edit preferences in settings.
  • Why do I have to be 13 or older? BuhEats includes user-generated content and is rated for ages 13+. Age is confirmed at sign-up.
