Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

BuhEats collects the following information to provide and improve our service:

  • Account Information: Email address, username, display name, and password (encrypted) required to create an account.
  • Profile Information: Optional profile picture, city, taste preferences (sweetness, ice level, milk base, favorite cuisines, dietary restrictions, spice tolerance).
  • Content You Create: Restaurant ratings, menu item reviews, photos (optional), tweets, visit history, and social interactions (follows, likes, comments).
  • Usage Data: App interactions and features used to improve the service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the BuhEats service
  • Process your reviews, ratings, and social features
  • Enable user profiles and social interactions (following, notifications)
  • Respond to your requests and customer support
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues and abuse

3. Data Storage & Third-Party Services

Your data is stored and processed using:

  • Convex: Our backend provider that stores all application data (users, reviews, restaurants, etc.). Data is transmitted securely and stored in compliance with industry standards.
  • Expo SecureStore: Stores your authentication token locally on your device.
  • Mapbox: Provides map displays and address lookup for restaurant locations. Map requests are handled by Mapbox; it is not used for advertising or tracking.
  • No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services are used in this app.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your content (reviews, ratings, profile, photos) is shared with other users as intended by the app's social features. We may access your data:

  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property
  • With your consent

5. Your Rights & Controls

You have the right to:

  • Access, update, or delete your account and data through the in-app Profile Settings
  • Delete your account entirely via Account Settings, which removes all your personal data from our servers
  • Opt out of emails or communications by contacting us

6. Data Retention

We retain your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, your personal information and user-generated content are permanently removed from our servers within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Children's Privacy

BuhEats is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. You must be at least 13 years old to create an account, confirmed at sign-up.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Significant changes will be notified in-app.

For questions about this policy or your data, contact us at:
